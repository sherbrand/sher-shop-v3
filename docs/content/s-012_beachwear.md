- H2: Frequently Asked Questions
- H3.1: {How do I measure for beachwear?}
- Paragraph.1: {Measure your bust, waist, and hip. Keep the tape level and snug, not tight. Bust matters most on a One Piece and on a bikini top. Hip matters most on a bikini bottom. Match your numbers to our size chart and pick the size that fits your largest one.}
- H3.2: {How should beachwear fit?}
- Paragraph.2: {Snug, not tight. It should hold you with no pulling at the seams and no skin pushed over an edge. Swim fabric gives a little once wet, so it should feel firm while it is dry. If it leaves marks or you cannot move freely, size up.}
- H3.3: {What if I am between sizes?}
- Paragraph.3: {Pick the size that fits your largest measurement. A set is sized as one piece, so the top and bottom come in the same size. If your bust and your hip land on different sizes, take the larger one.}
- H3.4: {How do I care for my beachwear?}
- Paragraph.4: {Rinse it in cold water after every wear, then hand wash it. Never dry clean it. Lay it flat to dry, out of direct sun. Always check the care label on your piece first, as fabrics differ.}
- H3.5: {How do I style beachwear?}
- Paragraph.5: {Wear it on its own at the water. To walk off the beach, pull a shirt or a skirt over it. A bikini top or a One Piece carries into the evening under an open shirt. Keep the jewelry small and let the piece lead.}
- H3.6: {Can I buy a bikini top or bottom on its own?}
- Paragraph.6: {No. Every bikini is sold as a set, top and bottom together.}
