## Across all products

### How to measure and pick a size
Variants: how do I measure myself / how do I measure bust waist and hips / where is my natural waist / how do I know my size / how does the size chart work / do you have a size guide
- Measure four points: bust, waist, hip, and length. Keep the tape level and
  snug, not tight.
- Match your numbers to the size chart on the product page. Pick the size that
  fits your largest measurement.

### If you are between sizes
Variants: I am between sizes / what if I fall between two sizes / should I size up or down / which size if I am in the middle
- Size up. Every SHER piece can be taken in but not let out, so the larger size
  gives you room to adjust.
- A tailor can take in the seams for a closer fit. Lace Closure corset tops can
  be cinched smaller without a tailor.

### What our clothes are made of
Variants: what fabric do you use / what is it made of / what material / is it real satin / what are the bones made of
- Weighted satin: the main fabric. It is heavy enough to drape clean and not
  cling.
- Satin lining: a smooth inner layer against the skin, for comfort and shape.
- [Flat steel] bones in fabric channels: the structure inside corset tops.
- Back lacing, metal eyelets, and separating zips: the closures on corset tops.
- Structured bust cups: pre-shaped panels that keep the front clean.
- Lace: cut and placed by hand so the pattern runs true.

### How to care for your piece
Variants: how do I wash it / can I machine wash / how do I dry it / can I dry clean / will the color fade
- Hand wash cold, or dry clean. This is safe for every piece.
- Do not machine wash corset tops: the spin bends the boning. Sets and dresses may
  be machine washed cold if the care label allows.
- Never wring satin. Twisting leaves marks that stay. Press the water out gently.
- Lay flat to dry, out of direct sun.
- Wash a matching set's two pieces together so they fade at the same rate.
- Always check the care label on your piece first, as fabrics differ.

### How to remove wrinkles and store it
Variants: how do I get wrinkles out of satin / can I iron satin / does satin wrinkle / how do I store it / how do I hang it
- Steam to remove wrinkles: hang the piece and hold the steamer 1 to 2 inches
  away.
- If you iron, turn the piece inside out, use the lowest heat, and lay a cloth on
  top.
- Store it hanging, so creases do not set.

## Corset Tops

### What a corset top is
Variants: what is a corset top / what does corset top mean / corset top meaning / is it a real corset / what does a corset top do / how is it different from a normal top
- A fitted top built on corsetry. Thin flat bones run through the fabric to shape
  the waist and support the bust.
- Unlike a plain top, it holds its structure and stays put as you move. Firm, but
  not stiff.
- Made to be worn on its own, as the piece people notice first.

### Is a corset top the same as a waist trainer
Variants: is it a waist trainer / will it make my waist smaller / do I size down / do I wear it a few hours at a time / is there a lace gap at the back
- No. A SHER corset top is a structured top, not a waist trainer.
- You do not size down for it, there is no fixed back gap to leave, and there is
  no break-in schedule. It should feel firm but never painful.

### Who a corset top suits
Variants: are corset tops flattering / who can wear a corset top / what body type suits a corset top / will it suit me
- It suits every body shape. The hand-set boning defines the waist and supports
  the bust, so it shapes a clean line on any figure.

### How SHER makes its corset tops different
Variants: why is SHER better / what makes it special / how is the boning done / why does it cost more
- The boning is set by hand. Each bone is placed one at a time on a mannequin, so
  it follows the curve of a real body.
- This takes longer and fits better: the top shapes you where it should and stays
  smooth where it should. That is why it reads elegant, not cheap.

### Corset top closures: Lace or Zip
Variants: lace or zip / what closures are there / which closure should I pick / how do you put on a corset top / how to lace a corset top / how does the zip work / which is easier to wear / is the fit different
- Lace Closure: laces up the back through metal eyelets. Pull the cord tighter or
  looser to set the fit yourself. Best if you want control, or a size change is
  likely.
- Zip Closure: a separating zip at the side or back. Step in, zip up, done. The
  fit is fixed. Best if you want the fastest way in and out.
- The shape is the same either way. Pick by preference.

### How a corset top should fit
Variants: how should it fit / how tight should it be / should a corset top be tight / does a corset top hurt / how do I know it fits / it feels too tight
- Firm but never painful. The top edge sits flat against the chest with no gap,
  and the waist holds close with no space to pinch.
- You should still breathe and move with ease. If it hurts or leaves marks, size
  up.
- Bust is the measurement that matters most.

### How to style a corset top
Variants: how do I wear it / how to wear a corset top with jeans / what do I wear with a corset top / corset top outfit ideas / can you wear a corset top casually
- Let the corset top lead. Keep the rest simple: plain trousers, a straight
  skirt, or tailored denim.
- One clean shoe and small jewelry is enough.

## Cocktail Dresses

### What a cocktail dress is
Variants: what is a cocktail dress / what does cocktail dress mean / what counts as a cocktail dress / what makes a dress a cocktail dress / what is it for
- A dressy short-to-mid dress made for evening events. It sits between everyday
  wear and a formal gown.
- Cut to skim the body in satin and clean lines. Sensual, but never cheap.

### Cocktail dress vs evening gown
Variants: cocktail dress vs evening gown / is a cocktail dress formal / is it the same as a party dress / what is the difference
- A cocktail dress is short-to-mid and sits below black tie. An evening gown is
  floor-length and formal.
- SHER's Maxi is the dressiest cocktail length, but it still reads cocktail, not a
  gown.

### Cocktail dress lengths: Mini, Midi, or Maxi
Variants: mini midi or maxi / mini vs midi vs maxi / what length should I pick / how long is a midi dress / what length cocktail dress / which length for my event
- Mini: hits the thigh. Short and striking, for a night out.
- Midi: falls between the knee and the ankle. The most versatile, right for
  dinners, parties, and daytime.
- Maxi: runs to the ankle or floor. The dressiest of the three, for grand events.

### What to wear a cocktail dress to
Variants: what event do I wear it to / is it ok for a wedding / what does cocktail attire mean / what time is cocktail attire / can I wear it to work
- Dressy events below black tie: parties, dinners, weddings as a guest, and work
  events with a dress code. When an invite says cocktail attire, this is the
  dress it means.
- Keep it cocktail: not too short or revealing, and not a floor-length gown.

### How a cocktail dress should fit
Variants: how should it fit / should it be tight / it feels tight across the hip
- It should skim the body, not grip it. The fabric follows your shape with no
  pulling and no horizontal creases across the bust or hip.
- You can sit, move, and breathe with ease. If you see tight lines forming, size
  up. A dress has to fit every point at once, so size to your largest
  measurement.

### How to style a cocktail dress
Variants: how do I style it / what shoes / what accessories
- Let the dress lead and add one strong accessory, like a bold earring or a
  clutch.
- Match the shoe to the hem: a heel for a Mini, a strappy sandal for a Midi, a
  fine heel for a Maxi. Keep everything else quiet.
