# Competitor Research

What the competitors marked Marketing in brand-sher.md actually do: where they
come from, what they charge, how they got an audience, and what they run as ads.

Checked 9 August 2026. Prices and ad counts drift. Re-check before acting on
numbers.

Brand-sher.md owns who to track. This file owns what they do.

## 1. Who they are

Not one is a Western brand. Two are in Bangkok.

| stolenstores.com | Bangkok | 2019 | Sisters Sanancaht and Onarin Siriviroj |
| cherieattire.com | Bangkok | 2020 | Helena Busch (Swedish, ex-model) and Nutrada Sottivoranan (Thai) |
| fanciclub.com | Vietnam | 2018 | Duy Tran, age 19, fashion-school dropout |

| perfectcorsetny.com | Ads paid by a Hong Kong company | Not found | Not found |

Perfect Corset trades as a New York label. Google's verified advertiser record
for the domain names YINO (HONGKONG) DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED.

Cherie and Stolen Stores work together. The Cherie founders name Stolen Stores
as a peer they share photoshoots and advice with.

## 3. How each one got an audience

Every one of them put product on someone whose photo travels. They used
different doors.

**Fanci Club: Western celebrities, unpaid.** Bella Hadid posted herself wearing
two of their upcycled pieces on her own account. Since then: Kylie Jenner,
Hailey Bieber, Dua Lipa, Olivia Rodrigo, Doja Cat, and Lisa and Jennie of
Blackpink. One pink-bowed black corset was worn by both Olivia Rodrigo and Im
Na-yeon.

**Stolen Stores: Western celebrities.** Sabrina Carpenter wore a cream heart
bralette on the UK leg of her tour. Press also reports Ariana Grande, Lisa, and
Elsa Hosk, though those names come from search summaries, not from articles read
in full.

**Cherie: influencer seeding during COVID.** They sent product to influencers
who were making content at home. The founders call influencers "connections,"
not a media buy. 95% of their customers are outside Thailand. The articles do
not name the influencers or say where they were based.

**Bupbes: K-pop stage wear.** On 2 June 2023 (G)I-DLE performed "Queencard" on
Music Bank in a full Bupbes outfit, and Vietnamese fans identified it. aespa and
Blackpink's Rosé have also worn the brand.

**Perfect Corset: paid ads.** No celebrity or creator appears in its ads.

### How people find out which brand it is

- Fans identify it and trace it back. K-pop has organised sites that catalogue
  idol outfits.
- The creator or their stylist tags the brand.
- Fashion press names the piece.
- The brand credits the handle in its own posts and ads.

Announcement bar reads "SUMMER STARTS WITH 20% OFF | CODE: SUMMER20" and

### The pattern

The premium brands advertise product. The cheap one advertises price. Across
every premium ad read, not one mentions a discount, a price cut, or a deadline.

## 6. What none of them do

Checked across all five homepages.

- **No fit tools.** Not one has a size finder. Stolen Stores has a sizing guide
  and made-to-measure, which is the strongest fit answer in the group.
- **Almost no reviews.** Cherie shows one rating of 4.3 from 3 reviews. Nobody
  else shows any.
- **No email offer.** Bupbes has a bare "Enter your email JOIN" with no reason
  to join. Nobody gives one.
- **No urgency or press.** No timers, no stock counters, no press logos, no
  named celebrities on any homepage.
- **Google is open.** The most any of them has ever run is 71 ads.
